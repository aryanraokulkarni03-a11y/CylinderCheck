# Bangalore First Supplier Playbook

## Positioning
- Household message: `Check before you book`
- Business message: `Compare tracked 19kg prices and browse suppliers`

## First supplier goal
- 20 Bangalore supplier contacts
- 5 real conversations
- 2 verification-ready suppliers
- 1 supplier live on CylinderCheck

## Founding listing offer
- Free founding listing for 60 days
- Verified badge after business and contact checks
- Direct inbound contact from buyers
- No commission in v1

## Qualification checklist
- Active business serving Bangalore
- Reachable phone number
- Clear city or area coverage
- Commercial LPG fit for restaurants, caterers, cloud kitchens, or hotels

## Direct-call opener
Hi, I run CylinderCheck. We track LPG market prices and are building a simple supplier directory for businesses that need 19kg LPG. I’d like to list verified suppliers in Bangalore and send direct buyer calls to you.

## Proof point
- City-level commercial LPG prices are already live on the site
- The commercial page is now a browse-first supplier directory, not a waitlist

## Reminder product note
- Public reminders are email-first in v1
- Paid Plus stays dark until reminder delivery is reliable

## Ask on the first call
- Which Bangalore areas do you serve?
- Do you currently supply 19kg commercial LPG?
- What number should buyers use to reach you directly?
- Would you like a free founding listing for 60 days?

## WhatsApp follow-up
Hi, this is {{your_name}} from CylinderCheck. We track commercial LPG prices and are listing verified Bangalore suppliers for business buyers. I’d like to add your business as a free founding listing for 60 days and send direct buyer calls to you. Site: https://www.cylindercheck.in/commercial

## Cadence
- Day 0: first call
- Day 0: WhatsApp follow-up
- Day 2: second call
- Day 5: final follow-up

## Notes to capture in the outreach sheet
- business name
- city/area coverage
- contact number
- decision maker
- first call result
- verification status
- next follow-up date

## Verification before listing
- Confirm business presence and service area
- Confirm direct phone number
- Confirm supplier category fits commercial LPG
- Confirm the listing should stay active
