## Route-level metadata checks
- unique title per indexable route
- unique description per indexable route
- canonical URL matches the route
- OG and Twitter tags use the brand lockup image
- structured data is route-specific

## Search Console checklist
- submit `https://www.cylindercheck.in/sitemap.xml`
- confirm canonical domain is `https://www.cylindercheck.in`
- watch impressions and clicks for:
  - Track
  - News
  - Commercial
  - Bangalore landing pages

## Content rhythm for the next 30-45 days
- 1 Bangalore-focused SEO page or update per week
- 2 short social/news recaps per week from the LPG news feed
- 1 supplier-proof update when the first verified supplier goes live

## Weekly review
- routes gaining impressions
- landing page click-through rate
- commercial page visits
- supplier calls or messages generated
- free email reminder signups
